Client questions

Frequently asked questions.

Answers about confidentiality, pricing, fit, independence, and focused reviews.

Can you sign a confidentiality agreement?

Yes. I am happy to sign a reasonable confidentiality or nondisclosure agreement before reviewing sensitive materials.

Can we start with a call?

Yes. A Scientific Advisory Call is $450 USD and includes a 60-minute discussion, limited advance review, and a brief written summary. The fee is credited toward a written review started within 7 days.

What does a written review cost?

A Focused Claim Review starts at $1,250 USD, an Executive Scientific Review starts at $2,500 USD, and Full Scientific Diligence starts at $5,000 USD. The exact fixed fee is confirmed after the materials and deadline are reviewed.

Do you work with non-scientists?

Yes. Deliverables are designed to be technically defensible while remaining usable by executives, investors, founders, advisors, and other non-specialist stakeholders.

Can you provide a positive endorsement?

No conclusion is guaranteed in advance. The engagement is an independent review, and the written findings reflect the evidence as assessed.

Can you review a single paper or claim?

Yes. A tightly defined question may be appropriate for a Focused Claim Review rather than a larger diligence engagement.
